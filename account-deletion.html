Delete your WortPop account and data

WortPop users can delete their account from the Account screen inside the app.

If you no longer have access to WortPop, request deletion by emailing nicolas.mori@gmail.com from the email address associated with your WortPop account. Include “WortPop account deletion” in the subject.

The request covers the WortPop account and associated application data. If any information must be retained for security or legal reasons, that retention will be described in the published privacy policy.
