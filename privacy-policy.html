WortPop Privacy Policy

WortPop uses account information for authentication and stores gameplay/statistics data to provide and synchronize the learning experience. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may process app interaction, device/app-instance and diagnostic information for analytics and reliability.

WortPop does not require precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, health, payment, SMS or call-log data for core functionality and does not sell user data.

Users can delete their account in the app or use the separate account-deletion resource. For privacy questions contact nicolas.mori@gmail.com.
